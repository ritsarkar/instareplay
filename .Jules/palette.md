## 2024-05-15 - ARIA Labels and Focus States on Topbar Buttons\n**Learning:** Interactive elements in the Topbar (like the bell icon and profile button) were missing explicit ARIA labels and relied on `focus:outline-none` without `focus-visible` fallback, breaking keyboard accessibility and screen reader experience.\n**Action:** Always add `aria-label` to icon-only buttons and replace `focus:outline-none` with `focus:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2` to ensure keyboard focus states are maintained.
