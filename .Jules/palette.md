## 2024-03-20 - Dynamic ARIA Labels in Lists\n**Learning:** When adding ARIA labels to icon-only action buttons inside tables or lists (like a delete button for a specific item), it's critical to interpolate the item's identifying information (e.g., `aria-label={"Delete " + doc.title}`) to provide adequate context for screen reader users, rather than a generic "Delete" label.\n**Action:** Always check the surrounding mapping context when adding ARIA labels in lists and use dynamic interpolation to ensure uniqueness and clarity.
